Christlike ≠ God incarnate

A person can reflect Jesus’ love. Only Jesus is the eternal Son of God made human.

Catholic teaching · CCC 461–464

Student activity

Investigate a figure. Explain the resemblance.

Work in pairs or a group of three. Choose one historical, biblical, mythical, legendary, or pop culture figure. Use their profile and your Unit 1.4 notes to answer the original three questions.

  1. Explore: read a profile and select a figure.
  2. Investigate: discuss evidence, then write your group’s answers about service, moral courage, and virtues.
  3. Make the distinction: explain why Christlike qualities do not make someone God incarnate.
  4. Present: share your findings and answers with the class. Every member contributes.

Profiles are starting points, not model answers. Name the action or scene, explain its meaning, and connect it to Jesus. Christlike comparisons are interpretations that need reasons.
